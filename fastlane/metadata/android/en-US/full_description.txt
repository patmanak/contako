Manage your Proton contacts on Android and make their supported fields available to your contacts, phone, messaging and mail apps.

• Browse and search contacts offline.
• Create and edit contacts with photos and repeated fields.
• Manage Proton groups for individual email addresses.
• Filter missing phone/email, local-only fields, recent changes and alias-only contacts.
• Inspect synchronization issues, compare conflicting versions and retry one affected item.
• Recover local edits after a contact is deleted on Proton.

Android receives supported fields; its group memberships follow the preferred email address. Additional imported data is retained during compatible edits.

Requires Android 12 or later and a Proton account. One account is supported at a time. Group availability depends on the account. Background synchronization timing depends on Android.

Free software, without advertising or analytics. Contako is an independent project and is not affiliated with Proton.
