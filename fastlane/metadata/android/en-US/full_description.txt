Browse, create and edit your Proton contacts and groups directly in Contako.

Contako syncs your Proton contacts with the Android system, so you can find and edit them in your usual contacts app, such as Google Contacts or Samsung Contacts.

Your contacts also become available to phone, SMS, email and messaging apps with permission to access your contacts.

Browse and search your contacts even offline, and add photos and multiple contact details to your cards.

Requires Android 12 or later and a Proton account. One account is supported at a time. Group availability depends on the account.

Free software, without advertising or analytics. Contako is an independent project and is not affiliated with Proton.
